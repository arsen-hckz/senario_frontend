3. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The site stores only what it needs to work, in your browser’s local storage: your sign-in session (if you sign in) and your cart. This storage is strictly necessary for services you ask for, so no consent is required; clearing your browser data removes it. The payment page of Viva Wallet sets its own cookies under its own policy.

4. Who we share data with

Only with the providers we need to run the shop, under contracts that protect your data:

  • Viva Payments S.A. (Greece) — card payments; acts as an independent controller for payment data.
  • [Courier name] — your name, address and phone, to deliver your parcel.
  • DigitalOcean — server hosting in Frankfurt, Germany (EU), where our database is stored.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Unfinished sign-ups (email never verified): deleted after 3 days.
  • Orders and receipts: as long as Greek tax law requires (currently at least 5 years from the end of the year of purchase).
  • Server logs and backups: up to 14 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Email Senarioproject@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr, Kifisias 1-3, 115 23 Athens).

7. Security

The site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored hashed, access to our systems is restricted, and data is backed up daily.
