Refunds

We refund the price of the returned items and the original standard delivery charge within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal notice. We may wait until the items arrive back (or you send proof of shipping). Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with, via Viva Wallet; your bank may take a few more days to show them. If you return only part of an order that had free delivery, the free-delivery threshold is not re-applied.

Faulty or wrong item

Contact us at Senarioproject@gmail.com with a photo. We cover the return cost and repair, replace or refund the item under the two-year legal guarantee.
